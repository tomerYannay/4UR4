> ## Standing authoring rule — **generalization requires measurement**
>
> **Adopted 2026-07-28** on the Strategic Product Reviewer's ruling, after the **same defect
> shape recurred seven times across PRs #38 and #41** and was caught by the next reader every
> single time, never by the writer. All seven are one shape: **a specific measured fact
> generalized beyond its support.**
>
> 1. **Universal quantifiers over a countable set** — *every, all, only, none, never, last,
>    the whole* — **require the measured count beside them.** Not "every candidate", but
>    "eight of the eighteen, measured".
> 2. **Negative capability claims** — *cannot, has never been able to, has no access* —
>    **require a configuration citation or a recorded attempt.** **A refused command is
>    evidence about the command string. It is never evidence about the role.**
>
> **The seven instances**, so the rule has something concrete behind it rather than being a
> slogan: *"every other HD in this register cites an artifact"* (false for ten) · *"three were
> falsified by the merge-forward"* (zero were) · *"no commit in this branch's ancestry"* (false
> under the whole-ancestry reading) · *"the last surviving instance in `engine/`"* (two
> survived) · *"only §2, §3 and §4 are transcribed"* (two dispositions inside them were not) ·
> *"every candidate is on §3's still-frozen list"* (false for ten of eighteen) · *"never been
> able to read the guard at any SHA"* (**false**, and endorsed by a gate before Code Review
> disproved it by performing the read).
>
> **Scope and status.** This is **authoring guidance**, not a `GOV-###` rule: no agent may
> create or amend a governance rule ([GOV-013](../governance/approval-gate.md) clause 5). It
> binds by convention and by review. **Promoting it into `GOVERNANCE.md` is a Product Owner
> decision**, and it is worth taking, because the class is currently caught only by whichever
> reader happens to check.
